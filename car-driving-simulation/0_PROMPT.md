Keep the car visible in the foreground while the background scenery scrolls continuously to create the feeling that the car is driving forward. Animate the wheels spinning realistically and add subtle movement to the car body so that the car feels connected to the road. Let the environment flow smoothly behind it, with repeating but varied scenery that makes the movement believable. Use cinematic lighting and a cohesive sky, such as sunset, twilight, or daylight, to enhance the atmosphere. The overall movement should appear calm, immersive, and realistic, with a continuous looping animation.
